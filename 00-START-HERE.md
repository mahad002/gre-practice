# GRE prep: start here

**Planned test:** Saturday, 23 January 2027 (confirm a seat before treating this date as final)  
**Target:** 325+ · Quant 165+ · Verbal 160+ · Analytical Writing 4.5+

This is your main self-study course: concept lessons, original drills with explanations, 600 vocabulary cards, writing practice, test-review systems and a dated plan. It is designed so that you always know what to do next.

The course cannot replace official ETS questions. Use the material here to learn and drill; use official ETS tests and books to calibrate your real score. Start with `01-Plan/Resources.md` before buying anything.

## Do these four things first

- [ ] Read `01-Plan/GRE-Plan.md` and confirm that 23 January fits your application timeline.
- [ ] In your ETS account, check **live** Islamabad test-centre availability and book the preferred date or a nearby date. A date or centre mentioned in this folder is not a reservation.

- [ ] Open `01-Plan/Study-Schedule.md` and complete today's row.

## Your daily loop

1. **Vocab — 25 minutes:** 20 new words until Day 30, then Anki reviews only.
2. **Quant — 60 minutes:** learn, solve without notes, then check.
3. **Verbal — 35 minutes:** use the stated method and prove each answer from the sentence or passage.
4. **Review — 10 minutes:** log every wrong answer, lucky guess and slow correct answer in `06-Practice-Tests/Error-Log.md`.
5. **Update — 5 minutes:** tick the schedule and update `01-Plan/Progress-Dashboard.md` on Sundays.

Most weekdays take about **2 hours 15 minutes**. If a day collapses, do Anki and review five error-log items; resume the schedule the next day. Do not try to repay an entire missed day at midnight.

## The source-quality rule

Use materials in this order:

1. **Official ETS timed tests** — score prediction and final decisions.
2. **Official ETS books and free PDFs** — realistic question practice.
3. **This folder** — teaching, repetition, targeted drilling and tracking.
4. **Third-party material** — extra Quant volume or a different explanation only; do not use its Verbal score to predict your GRE.

## Five non-negotiable habits

1. Do vocabulary reviews every day.
2. Attempt a question before reading its solution.
3. Log wrong, guessed and slow questions—not just wrong ones.
4. Redo every logged question twice on different days before marking it fixed.
5. Spend at least as long reviewing a full mock as you spent taking it.

## Honest expectation

No folder can guarantee a score. A 325+ result depends on your starting point, consistency, timed accuracy and official-mock results. This course gives you the system; the decision rules in `06-Practice-Tests/Mock-Test-Guide.md` tell you when the evidence supports keeping or moving the test date.
