# 12. Mixed Timed Quant Sets

Six original sets in the format of the shorter GRE: 12 questions in 21 minutes (section 1) or 15 questions in 26 minutes (section 2).

How to use them:
- Set a strict timer and stop when it ends. No pausing.
- Use only a basic four-function calculator with square root, like the on-screen one. Use scratch paper, not the margins of this file.
- Mark and return: if a question is not moving after about 2 minutes, guess, mark it, and move on.
- Never leave a blank. There is no penalty for a wrong answer.
- After each set, check the answers at the bottom of this file and log every miss (and every lucky guess) in `06-Practice-Tests/Error-Log.md`.

Pacing guide: about 1 min 45 s per question. Aim to finish a first pass through everything in about 70 percent of the time (about 15 minutes of 21, or about 18 minutes of 26), then spend the rest on marked questions.

Quantitative Comparison (QC) choices are always the same:
A = Quantity A is greater. B = Quantity B is greater. C = The two quantities are equal. D = The relationship cannot be determined from the information given.

For numeric entry, type a number or a fraction. Equivalent fractions count as correct. For "select all that apply", one or more options are correct and you need the complete set.

## Answers and solutions

The topic in brackets tells you which lesson file to revisit.

### Set 1

Key: 1 C, 2 B, 3 A, 4 B, 5 D, 6 B, 7 C, 8 40, 9 D, 10 C, 11 96, 12 A B E

1. C [Arithmetic: remainders and cycles]. 7 has the same remainder as 2 when divided by 5, and powers of 2 cycle 2, 4, 3, 1 every 4, so 7^20 leaves 1. Units digits of powers of 3 cycle 3, 9, 7, 1, so 3^20 ends in 1 and leaves 1. Equal.
2. B [Percents]. Multiply: 1.2 x 0.8 = 0.96, so the final price is 96 percent of the original. An increase and decrease by the same percent never returns you to the start.
3. A [Algebra: inequalities and signs]. xy < 0 means opposite signs, and x > y then forces x > 0 > y. A minus B is (x - y) - (x + y) = -2y, which is positive. A is greater.
4. B [Geometry: circles and squares]. Radius is 5, so the circle's area is 25*pi, about 78.5. The square's side is 9, area 81.
5. D [Statistics: mean and median]. The median is 5 for every positive integer x. The mean is (21 + x)/5, which equals 5 when x = 4 and is more than 5 when x = 10. Two different relationships, so D.
6. B [Exponents]. Factor: 2^x (2^3 - 1) = 7 * 2^x = 112, so 2^x = 16 and x = 4.
7. C [Word problems: work]. Combined rate is 1/6 + 1/9 = 5/18 per hour, so 2 hours finishes 5/9. The remaining 4/9 at B's rate of 1/9 per hour takes 4 hours.
8. 40 [Counting]. Choose 2 of 5 women (10 ways) and 1 of 4 men (4 ways): 10 x 4 = 40.
9. D [Data interpretation: percent change]. Furniture: 50/200 = 25 percent. Electronics: 60/480 = 12.5 percent. Grocery: 60/600 = 10 percent. Clothing and Toys fell.
10. C [Data interpretation: percent of total]. 660/1,874 is about 0.352, so about 35 percent.
11. 96 [Data interpretation: repeated percent change]. Toys fell from 150 to 120, which is a 20 percent decrease. Another 20 percent decrease: 120 x 0.8 = 96.
12. A, B, E [Number properties: prime factorization]. 72 = 2^3 * 3^2. Exponents in n^2 are even, so n^2 needs at least 2^4 * 3^2, which means n is a multiple of 2^2 * 3 = 12. So 4, 6, and 12 must divide n. n = 12 shows 8, 9, and 24 need not.

### Set 2

Key: 1 B, 2 C, 3 A, 4 D, 5 B, 6 C, 7 B, 8 D, 9 A E F, 10 79, 11 D, 12 C, 13 B, 14 D, 15 12/67

1. B [Ratios]. A part-to-part ratio of 3 to 5 means boys are 3/8 of the class, which is less than 3/5.
2. C [Roots]. sqrt(50) = 5*sqrt(2) and sqrt(18) = 3*sqrt(2), sum 8*sqrt(2). sqrt(128) = 8*sqrt(2). Equal. (Calculator check: both are about 11.31.)
3. A [Algebra: comparing fractions]. Rewrite as 1 + 1/x and 1 + 1/(x + 1). Since x < x + 1 and both are positive, 1/x is larger. A is greater.
4. D [Geometry: triangle inequality]. AC must be between 7 - 5 = 2 and 7 + 5 = 12. It could be 3, 6, or 10, so D.
5. B [Probability: binomial]. There are 16 outcomes and C(4, 2) = 6 have exactly 2 heads, so the probability is 6/16 = 3/8, less than 1/2. The trap is assuming "half heads" has probability 1/2.
6. C [Statistics: standard deviation]. Multiplying every value by 2 doubles the standard deviation to 8. Subtracting 5 shifts every value and does not change the spread.
7. B [Percents: markup and discount]. Take cost = 100. Marked price 160, sale price 160 x 0.75 = 120. Profit is 20 percent of cost.
8. D [Functions]. f(3) = 9 - 12 + k = 2 gives k = 5. Then f(-1) = 1 + 4 + 5 = 10. Watch the sign: -4 times -1 is +4.
9. A, E, F [Inequalities: absolute value]. 2x - 3 > 5 gives x > 4, and 2x - 3 < -5 gives x < -1. Only -3, 4.5, and 6 qualify. The endpoints -1 and 4 give exactly 5, which is not greater than 5.
10. 79 [Sequences]. Four steps from the 3rd to the 7th term add 16, so d = 4. The 20th term is 13 steps after the 7th: 27 + 13 x 4 = 79.
11. D [Coordinate geometry: lines]. Slope is (-3 - 5)/(6 - 2) = -2. From (2, 5), y must drop by 5, so x rises by 2.5 to 4.5.
12. C [Word problems: mixtures]. Salt: 0.15(40) + 0.30x = 0.20(40 + x), so 6 + 0.3x = 8 + 0.2x and x = 20. Shortcut: 15 is 5 below the target and 30 is 10 above, so the volumes are in the ratio 10 to 5, which gives 40 to 20.
13. B [Data interpretation: percent of a column]. Use the Over 15 km column total: 50/132 is about 37.9 percent. Dividing by the train row total (80) gives the trap 62.5 percent.
14. D [Data interpretation: probability with overlap]. Car 160 plus under 5 km 110 minus the overlap 30 = 240. 240/400 = 3/5.
15. 12/67 [Data interpretation: fractions]. Bicycle commuters at 15 km or less: 35 + 13 = 48. All commuters at 15 km or less: 110 + 158 = 268. 48/268 = 12/67.

### Set 3

Key: 1 D, 2 A, 3 B, 4 A, 5 D, 6 C, 7 A B D F, 8 B, 9 19/66, 10 C, 11 E, 12 A B D

1. D [Number properties: remainders]. Test values: n = 7 leaves 7 when divided by 8, and n = 19 leaves 3. One is more than 5 and one is less, so D.
2. A [Inequalities: absolute value]. 1 < x < 5 and -2 < y < 0. The product is negative, and its size is less than 5 x 2 = 10 because neither endpoint is reached. So xy > -10 always.
3. B [Geometry: inscribed rectangle]. The diagonal of the rectangle is a diameter, 10. The other side is sqrt(100 - 36) = 8, so the area is 48, less than 50.
4. A [Statistics: normal distribution]. 58 is 1.5 standard deviations below the mean and 86 is 2 standard deviations above. By symmetry, the tail beyond 1.5 standard deviations is bigger than the tail beyond 2. No table needed.
5. D [Percents: overlapping groups]. Take 100 employees: 40 engineers, of whom 10 are managers. Total managers 16, so 6 managers among the 60 non-engineers: 6/60 = 10 percent. The trap is answering 6 percent.
6. C [Exponents]. Three equal terms: 3 * 3^(x+1) = 3^(x+2). Right side: 9^(x-2) = 3^(2x-4). So x + 2 = 2x - 4 and x = 6.
