# GRE error log

Log every wrong answer, lucky guess and correct answer that was unacceptably slow. The goal is not to collect mistakes; it is to close them.

## Error codes

- **Concept:** did not know or recall the rule.
- **Process:** knew the content but used a weak method or skipped a needed step.
- **Timing:** method was valid but too slow, or the section plan failed.
- **Careless:** misread, copied, calculated or entered incorrectly.
- **Trap:** accepted an answer that did not answer the exact question.
- **Vocab:** a word or secondary meaning blocked the answer.

## Closure rule

An item is fixed only after two correct, unaided re-solves on different days:

1. **Redo 1:** 2–3 days after the miss.
2. **Redo 2:** 7–10 days after the miss, mixed with other questions.

If either redo fails, write a better prevention rule and restart the two-redo cycle.
