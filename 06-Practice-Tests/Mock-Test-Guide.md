# Official practice tests: sequence, review and decisions

Official tests are the calibration layer of this course. Original questions in the folder are for learning and drilling; an unofficial score is not evidence that you are ready for 325+.

## 1. Test sequence

| Date | Test | What you record | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 28 Nov | **POWERPREP 1** (free) | Raw correct/attempted by section; no official V/Q scaled score because this product is untimed | Interface, question types and baseline error pattern |
| 26 Dec | **POWERPREP PLUS 1** (USD 39.95 listed) | V, Q, AW, section data and error types | First scaled official checkpoint |
| 9 Jan | **POWERPREP 2** (free, timed) | V, Q, raw/section data and provisional essay review | Main keep/reschedule decision |
| 16 Jan | **POWERPREP PLUS 2** (USD 39.95 listed) | V, Q, AW, section data and error types | Dress rehearsal at the real appointment time |
| Optional | **POWERPREP PLUS 3** | Same fields | Use only if another official data point will change a decision; do not cram it into the final four days |

Get the products through the [official POWERPREP page](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/powerprep.html) or your ETS account. Product details and prices can change.

## 2. Conditions

- Use one uninterrupted sitting and the official section times: AW 30, V 18/23 and Q 21/26 minutes.
- For the untimed POWERPREP 1 product, impose those limits yourself if the interface permits and record where time expires. ETS still will not provide a V/Q scaled score for the untimed product.
- Write the essay; skipping it makes the remaining test easier than the real experience.
- Use only allowed scratch materials and the on-screen calculator.
- Put the phone away, silence notifications and do not pause for explanations.
- From January, test at the same time of day as the real appointment.

## 3. Review protocol

Review the next day for 2–3 hours.

1. Before reading explanations, redo every wrong, guessed and slow question untimed.
2. For each item, identify the first broken step—not merely the topic.
3. Classify it in `Error-Log.md`: **Concept, Process, Timing, Careless, Trap or Vocab**.
4. Write a one-line prevention rule that can be used on a different question.
5. Read the official explanation and compare methods.
6. Schedule redo 1 for 2–3 days later and redo 2 for 7–10 days later.
7. Count losses by category and topic; update `01-Plan/Progress-Dashboard.md`.

## 5. Reading the evidence

- Do not convert raw misses to a homemade scaled score. GRE scoring depends on section-level adaptation and equating.
- Treat one mock as a sample, not a destiny. Look for the pattern across official timed results.
- A high total can hide a section problem. For this plan, Q165+ and V160+ both matter.
- A correct guess and a correct answer that took over roughly 2.5 minutes are review items.
- Section 1 deserves controlled accuracy because it determines the difficulty of section 2, but every scored question contributes to the result.

## 6. Decision points

### After POWERPREP PLUS 1 on 26 December

- **323+ with Q164+ and V158+:** stay on plan; repair the top two leaks.
- **316–322:** stay on plan for now, but replace two weekly drill blocks with the highest-loss topics.
- **315 or below, or either section below 155:** identify whether the gap is concept or timing. If 325+ is essential and the application timeline allows flexibility, start checking February seats; make the final call after POWERPREP 2.

### After timed POWERPREP 2 on 9 January

- **323+ and neither section more than 2 points below its target:** keep 23 January.
- **318–322:** 325+ is possible but not secure. Keep the date only if the last two official results are rising and the misses are concentrated in fixable careless/timing errors; otherwise reschedule.
- **Below 318, Q below 160 or V below 155:** if 325+ is the actual requirement and you have timeline flexibility, rescheduling is the evidence-based choice.

### After POWERPREP PLUS 2 on 16 January

- Use it to tune pacing and confidence, not to launch a new curriculum.
- If the result collapses because of illness, interruption or a clear one-off event, compare it with 9 January before deciding.
- The usual reschedule deadline is four days before the appointment; confirm the exact deadline and available dates in the ETS account.

## 7. Ask Codex for analysis, not an invented score

Useful prompt: “Read my score table and error log. Separate knowledge gaps from execution gaps, estimate which two repairs have the highest point value, and rewrite only the next seven days of the schedule.”

Any essay score from an AI is provisional. Compare the feedback with the [official ETS Issue scoring guide](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/content/analytical-writing/scoring.html).
