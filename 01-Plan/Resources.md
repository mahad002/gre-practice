# GRE resources: what to use and what to skip

Checked 23 November 2026. Prices and product details can change; confirm them in your ETS account before paying.

## 1. Official resources: use these first

| Resource | Current role | Cost | Planned use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| [POWERPREP 1](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/powerprep.html) | Full official **untimed** test. ETS does not provide V/Q scaled scores for the untimed test. Use it to learn the interface and record raw accuracy. | Free | 28 Nov |
| [POWERPREP 2](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/powerprep.html) | Full official timed test with V/Q scores. Save it for the main readiness decision. | Free | 9 Jan |
| POWERPREP PLUS 1 and 2 | Full official timed tests with scores, explanations, difficulty information and scored writing. ETS lists three versions, sold separately. | **USD 39.95 each** on the current ETS page | 26 Dec and 16 Jan |
| [ETS Math Review](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare.html) | Free official review of the math content; use it to check for gaps, not as the main drill source. | Free | Weeks 6–7 |
| [ETS Quant overview and sample questions](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/content/quantitative-reasoning.html) | Official question types, calculator guidance and samples. | Free | Week 1 and weak-topic review |
| [ETS Verbal overview](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/content/verbal-reasoning.html) | Official descriptions and samples for TC, SE and RC. | Free | Week 1 |
| [ETS Issue topic pool](https://www.ets.org/pdfs/gre/issue-pool.pdf) | The published pool from which the Issue task is selected. | Free | Four essays + prompt planning |
| [ETS Issue scoring guide](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/content/analytical-writing/scoring.html) | The standard for self-review and tutor feedback. | Free | Every essay |

### Important POWERPREP distinction

Do not write a fake scaled score for POWERPREP 1. Record raw correct/attempted counts by section and the error types. Your first **scaled** official checkpoint is POWERPREP PLUS 1 on 26 December. POWERPREP 2 is the free **timed** test and should remain unused until 9 January.

## 2. Official books: recommended for weeks 5–8

- **Official GRE Verbal Reasoning Practice Questions** — highest-value book because realistic Verbal questions are difficult to imitate.
- **Official GRE Quantitative Reasoning Practice Questions** — useful for timed official blocks and mixed practice.
- **The Official Guide to the GRE General Test** — additional official questions and explanations.

They are often bundled as the **Official GRE Super Power Pack**. Compare the bundle price with individual print or eBook prices. Some editions were designed around the longer pre-September-2023 test, but the question types and measured skills remain relevant; follow the **current 12/15-question timings in this folder**, not an old full-test layout from a book.

## 3. Optional supplements

| Resource | Use only when | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| GregMat+ or another structured video course | A lesson here does not click and you need a different teacher | Verify the current price yourself; do not run two complete study plans at once. |
| Manhattan 5 lb. | Q is below target and you need more topic-specific volume | Best for Quant volume; do not treat its Verbal as official calibration. |
| Khan Academy | A basic algebra, geometry or statistics idea needs slower explanation | Map the lesson back to the GRE topic before drilling broadly. |
| [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) | Daily spaced-repetition vocabulary review | Desktop is free; the official iOS app is paid. Avoid similarly named clone apps. |

## 4. Spend money in this order

1. GRE registration.
2. Two POWERPREP PLUS tests (about **USD 79.90 total** before any applicable taxes).
3. Official Verbal book.
4. Official Quant book and Official Guide, preferably as a cost-effective bundle.
5. A one-month video course only if a specific learning gap remains.

## 5. Avoid these traps

- Collecting resources instead of finishing questions.
- Using an unofficial mock score to predict your real Verbal result.
- Learning thousands of words shallowly. Master the 600 here, then add words only from official questions and your reading.
- Taking full tests without spending 2–3 hours reviewing them.
- Buying new material in the final two weeks.
- Copying institution codes from old web pages. Confirm recipients in the live ETS search on test day.

The usual rescheduling deadline is no later than four days before the appointment (for a Saturday test, Tuesday). Confirm the exact deadline in your ETS account.

## 7. Use Codex selectively

Good uses:

- explain a logged mistake in a second way;
- generate a small set of clearly labelled **non-official** drills for one weak concept;
- quiz vocabulary from named Anki tags;
- grade an essay provisionally against the ETS rubric;
- analyze the error log and rebalance future blocks.

Do not ask any AI to reproduce copyrighted ETS questions or to invent an “official” scaled score.
