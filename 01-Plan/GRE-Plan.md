## 2. Current GRE structure

The computer-delivered GRE General Test takes about **1 hour 58 minutes** and has no scheduled break.

| Measure | Structure | Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Analytical Writing | One Analyze an Issue task | 30 minutes |
| Verbal Reasoning | 12 questions, then 15 questions | 18 minutes, then 23 minutes |
| Quantitative Reasoning | 12 questions, then 15 questions | 21 minutes, then 26 minutes |

Analytical Writing is first. The four Verbal and Quant sections may follow in any order. Verbal and Quant are **section-level adaptive**: performance in the first section determines the difficulty of the second section for that measure. You may skip, mark and return to questions within the current section. There is no penalty for a wrong answer.

Scores are V130–170, Q130–170 and AW0–6. Official scores normally appear **8–10 days** after the test and remain reportable for five years. ETS permits another GRE after 21 days, up to five attempts in a rolling 12-month period.

## 3. What the target means

Using ETS percentile data based on test takers from July 2022 through June 2025:

| Target | ETS percentile | Preparation implication |
|---|---:|---|
| Q165 | 67th | Quant is compressed at the top; accuracy and avoiding careless losses matter greatly. |
| V160 | 82nd | Strong vocabulary plus evidence-based reading is required. |
| AW4.5 | about 85th | A clear, developed response that follows the exact instruction is more valuable than a memorized template. |

There is no fixed public conversion from “questions missed” to a scaled score because section difficulty and equating matter. Use official timed tests—not a guessed miss count—to judge readiness.

## 5. How to adapt without destroying the plan

After every official mock:

1. Separate misses into **concept, process, timing, careless, trap and vocabulary**.
2. Rank topics by lost points, not by dislike.
3. Replace at most **two** ordinary study blocks in the next week with your two highest-loss topics.
4. Keep vocabulary, official timed work and mock review fixed.
5. Use the rules in `06-Practice-Tests/Mock-Test-Guide.md` before changing the test date.

## 6. After the test

- **325+ with the section balance you need:** stop unless a specific program clearly needs more.
- **320–324:** decide based on program expectations and your official-mock trend; a March–April retake remains possible.
- **Below 320:** review the diagnostic report and error log before booking a retake. Do not simply repeat the same schedule.
