## Weekly decision rule

Choose the next repair block using this order:

1. repeated **concept** error;
2. repeated **process/trap** error;
3. accuracy below target under timing;
4. isolated careless error;
5. topic you merely dislike.

Do not add more than two repair blocks to a week. Replace low-priority drills; keep official mocks, mock review, sleep and daily vocabulary.
