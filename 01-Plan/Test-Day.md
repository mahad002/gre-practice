# Test day: Sat 23 Jan 2027

## The week before
- [ ] Mon–Wed: light timed sets, error-log review, vocab reviews. No new topics.
- [ ] Check your appointment time and the test-centre address in your ETS account. Do a practice trip if you've never been there.
- [ ] Check that the first and last name on your ETS account match the valid physical ID you will use. For a Pakistani citizen testing in Pakistan, ETS currently lists a passport or SMART National Identity Card with name, photo and signature; recheck the live country rule.
- [ ] Decide your 4 included score recipients. Additional score reports currently cost **USD 40** each. Confirm every institution inside the ETS recipient search instead of relying on an old code.
- [ ] Sleep and wake at test-day times from Wednesday.

## The day before
- [ ] 30–45 minutes only: read `02-Quant/00-Formula-Sheet.md`, the "rule" column of your error log, and your prepared essay examples.
- [ ] Pack: your valid accepted **physical ID**, appointment confirmation, water and a snack (they stay outside the testing room), and a light layer.
- [ ] No practice test. No late night. In bed by 11 pm.

## On the day
- [ ] Eat a normal breakfast. Arrive **30 minutes early**.
- [ ] You'll get scratch paper and pencils at the centre. Phones and watches go in the locker.
- [ ] There is **no scheduled break** in the 1 hour 58 minute test. Use the toilet before you check in.

## During the test
1. **Essay (30 min):** 3–4 minutes of planning, write 5 paragraphs, keep 3 minutes to proofread.
2. **Section 1 of Verbal and Quant: accuracy first.** These decide how hard your second sections are.
3. **Pacing:** about 1.5 minutes per Verbal question and 1.75 minutes per Quant question. If you're stuck after 2 minutes: guess, **mark**, move on.
4. **Verbal order:** Sentence Equivalence and Text Completion first, then short passages, then the long passage.
5. **Quant:** on every question, re-read the last line before you confirm. What exactly is asked?
6. In the last minute of each section, make sure **every question has an answer**. There's no penalty for wrong answers.
7. If one section goes badly, let it go. You can't judge your score from how it feels, and a harder second section is a good sign.

## At the end
- You'll be asked whether to **report or cancel** before seeing the unofficial V/Q scores. In normal circumstances, choose **report**; cancel only if you understand the consequences and a serious test-day irregularity makes the result unusable. ScoreSelect later lets you choose which reportable test administrations to send, subject to any program-specific policy.
- You'll then see your unofficial Verbal and Quant scores on screen. Write them in `06-Practice-Tests/Mock-Test-Guide.md`.
- Official scores, with Writing, arrive in your ETS account in about 8–10 days.

## After
- **325+** → record the official result and update your application plan.
- **320–324** → optional retake in Mar–Apr 2027.
- **Under 320** → retake in Mar–Apr 2027. You can test again after 21 days, up to 5 times in 12 months.
