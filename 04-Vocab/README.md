# GRE Vocabulary: 600 words in 30 study days

**Files.** `Words-Week-1.md` to `Words-Week-6.md` hold 100 words each (5 days of 20), ordered from most common to hardest. `Quiz-Week-N.md` tests each week. `anki-import.tsv` holds the same 600 words as flashcards.

**Daily routine (about 30 minutes).**
1. Read today's 20 words. Each day is one meaning theme, so learn the words as a group of synonyms and opposites.
2. Say every example sentence aloud, then cover the Meaning column and test yourself.
3. Review yesterday's group and the group from the same day last week.
4. Do your Anki reviews. Each weekend, take that week's quiz and re-study every word you missed.

**Anki import.** Open Anki, choose File, then Import, and select `anki-import.tsv`. Set the separator to Tab, turn on "Allow HTML in fields", and map the columns to Front, Back and Tags. Cards are tagged `Day01` to `Day30`. Recommended settings: 20 new cards per day and a maximum of 200 reviews per day.
