# Quiz: Week 4 (Days 16 to 20)

25 questions on the 100 words in `Words-Week-4.md`. Cover the answers at the bottom, write your answers down, then check them. Aim for 20 or more correct.

## Part A: Fill the blank

Choose the one word that best completes the sentence.

1. Critics complained that the report was ______ with errors, finding at least one on almost every page.

   (A) commensurate  (B) placid  (C) bereft  (D) devoid  (E) replete

2. The lack of a visa ______ any possibility of her attending the conference in person.

   (A) fostered  (B) disseminated  (C) precluded  (D) facilitated  (E) augmented

3. Once the storm had ______, the fishermen went out to inspect the damage to their boats.

   (A) converged  (B) waxed  (C) galvanized  (D) proliferated  (E) abated

4. The punishment should be ______ with the offense: a small fine for a small fault, a heavy one for a grave one.

   (A) ubiquitous  (B) incongruous  (C) heterogeneous  (D) sullen  (E) commensurate

5. Even after losing the first two games, the coach remained ______, insisting that the team would still win the series.

   (A) sparse  (B) despondent  (C) querulous  (D) sanguine  (E) morose

6. Plastic bags have become so ______ that they are now found on remote islands and mountain tops.

   (A) scant  (B) ubiquitous  (C) disjointed  (D) sparse  (E) placid

7. To refuse to answer the committee's questions was ______ to an admission of guilt.

   (A) superfluous  (B) devoid  (C) tantamount  (D) bereft  (E) replete

8. The new software ______ the need for manual checks, since every error is now caught automatically.

   (A) encumbered  (B) obviated  (C) amplified  (D) hampered  (E) inundated

9. The ______ shopkeeper snapped at customers for the smallest reason.

   (A) placid  (B) serene  (C) jubilant  (D) irascible  (E) phlegmatic

10. A ______ of skilled nurses forced the hospital to close two of its wards.

   (A) surfeit  (B) plethora  (C) glut  (D) dearth  (E) dichotomy

## Part B: Pick the two synonyms

Choose the two words that both fit the sentence and give it the same meaning.

11. Bad weather ______ the search, and by nightfall the teams had covered only half the area.

   (A) juxtaposed  (B) hindered  (C) hampered  (D) expedited  (E) facilitated  (F) saturated

12. Public interest in the trial ______ as the weeks went by, and soon the courtroom was half empty.

   (A) proliferated  (B) waxed  (C) waned  (D) dwindled  (E) galvanized  (F) converged

13. There was a ______ of applicants for the post: more than nine hundred people applied for a single job.

   (A) glut  (B) plethora  (C) dearth  (D) dichotomy  (E) paucity  (F) paradox

14. The ministry used radio and newspapers to ______ the new safety advice to every village.

   (A) stifle  (B) disseminate  (C) curtail  (D) truncate  (E) satiate  (F) propagate

15. The ______ goalkeeper showed no reaction, whether his team scored or conceded.

   (A) imperturbable  (B) irascible  (C) jubilant  (D) petulant  (E) exuberant  (F) phlegmatic

16. The ______ old farmer quarreled with every visitor and chased most of them off his land.

   (A) irascible  (B) sanguine  (C) placid  (D) elated  (E) cantankerous  (F) serene

17. On such a ______ income, the family could afford meat only once a month.

   (A) profuse  (B) copious  (C) myriad  (D) scant  (E) pervasive  (F) meager

18. The team was ______ after its unexpected victory, singing all the way home.

   (A) despondent  (B) phlegmatic  (C) morose  (D) elated  (E) sparse  (F) jubilant

19. The editor cut every ______ sentence, keeping only what the argument needed.

   (A) superfluous  (B) extraneous  (C) analogous  (D) placid  (E) scant  (F) sanguine

20. The government sent troops to ______ the uprising before it could spread.

   (A) foster  (B) quell  (C) disseminate  (D) abet  (E) stifle  (F) augment

## Part C: Which word does not belong?

Four of the five words are close in meaning. Choose the one that is not.

21. (A) hinder  (B) expedite  (C) impede  (D) encumber  (E) hamper

22. (A) replete  (B) myriad  (C) profuse  (D) copious  (E) meager

23. (A) wane  (B) abate  (C) augment  (D) ebb  (E) subside

24. (A) melancholy  (B) ebullient  (C) despondent  (D) sullen  (E) morose

25. (A) permeate  (B) propagate  (C) diffuse  (D) disseminate  (E) truncate

## Answers

1. E, replete. An error on almost every page means the report was full of them.
2. C, precluded. Without a visa, attendance is made impossible in advance.
3. E, abated. They could go out only after the storm had become less intense.
4. E, commensurate. Small for small and heavy for grave means in proportion.
5. D, sanguine. Insisting on a win after two losses is optimism in a hard situation.
6. B, ubiquitous. Found even in remote places means found everywhere.
7. C, tantamount. Tantamount to means equal in effect to.
8. B, obviated. Automatic checking removes the need for manual checks.
9. D, irascible. Snapping for the smallest reason shows a quick temper.
10. D, dearth. Wards close when there is a shortage of nurses.
11. B and C, hindered and hampered. Covering only half the area shows the search was slowed. Expedited and facilitated are opposites.
12. C and D, waned and dwindled. A half-empty courtroom shows that interest decreased.
13. A and B, glut and plethora. Nine hundred applicants for one job is far more than enough. Dearth and paucity mean shortage.
14. B and F, disseminate and propagate. Radio and newspapers are used to spread information widely.
15. A and F, imperturbable and phlegmatic. Showing no reaction either way means not easily excited or upset.
16. A and E, irascible and cantankerous. Quarreling with everyone shows a bad temper.
17. D and F, scant and meager. Affording meat once a month means the income is very small.
18. D and F, elated and jubilant. Singing after a victory shows great joy.
19. A and B, superfluous and extraneous. What the argument does not need is unnecessary.
20. B and E, quell and stifle. Troops are sent to put down an uprising, not to help it.
21. B, expedite. The other four mean to slow down or obstruct. Expedite means to speed up.
22. E, meager. The other four suggest large amounts. Meager means small in amount.
23. C, augment. The other four mean to decrease. Augment means to increase.
24. B, ebullient. The other four describe gloom. Ebullient means cheerful and full of energy.
25. E, truncate. The other four mean to spread. Truncate means to cut short.
